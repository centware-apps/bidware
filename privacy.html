BidWare Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

BidWare is developed by CentWare. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the BidWare app and website. The short version: we do not collect your personal data.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter into BidWare — your jobs, costs, pricing targets, and the customer names and site addresses you type — is stored locally on your own device. It is never sent to CentWare, never uploaded to our servers, and we have no way to see it. We do not operate servers that receive your business information.

The Android app works fully offline

The BidWare Android app requests no permissions and needs no internet connection. Your quotes and PDF exports are created entirely on your device.

No accounts, no tracking

BidWare has no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Internet access (web and iPhone versions)

The web version of BidWare loads over the internet from GitHub Pages, plus a font stylesheet from Google Fonts. Those services may see your device's IP address as part of normal web delivery, the same as loading any web page. No information you type into BidWare is included in those requests.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
